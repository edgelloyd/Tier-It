Tier It Support

Frequently asked questions and support for Tier It on iOS & iPadOS.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import images?
Tap the + button in the bottom dock. You can import multiple photos directly from your Photos Library, or choose a folder or .zip archive from the Files app.
How do I rank items on the board?
You can drag the Next Up thumbnail directly onto any tier row or between existing items. Alternatively, tap any of the quick-rank buttons (S, A, B, C, D, F) at the bottom to assign the active item with one tap.
How do I move or unrank an item already on the board?
Simply tap and drag the item to a new position or tier. You can also tap the item to open the quick-action bar at the bottom, where you can tap a new tier, unrank it back to the queue, or delete it.
How do I customize tier labels or hide tiers?
Tap the ••• menu in the top right corner and select Tier Settings (or tap any tier label header directly). Here you can rename labels and toggle visibility for any tier.
How do I remove the export watermark?
Tap the ••• menu and select Remove Watermark to unlock clean, unbranded exports via a one-time in-app purchase.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open the ••• menu, tap Remove Watermark, and select Restore Purchases at the bottom of the sheet.
Is my data private?
Yes, 100%. All tier lists, images, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. Tier It has no ads, no trackers, and no external servers.
