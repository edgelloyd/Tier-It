# Privacy Policy for Tier It
*Last updated: October 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how **Tier It** ("the App") handles information. The developer values your privacy, and the App has been built with an offline-first architecture that collects no personal information.

---

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, analytics, or usage metrics. Specifically:

* **No Account Required:** No registration, email address, password, or name is ever requested.
* **No Telemetry or Tracking:** No device identifiers, IP addresses, diagnostic logs, or location data are gathered.
* **No Third-Party SDKs:** The App does not integrate third-party analytics (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics), crash trackers, or advertising frameworks.

---

### 2. On-Device Data Storage

All content created within the App—including tier lists, custom labels, categories, and imported images—is stored locally on your device. We do not operate external database servers and cannot view or access your data.

---

### 3. In-App Purchases

All optional in-app purchases and payments are processed exclusively through Apple's App Store via StoreKit and your Apple Account. 

The developer does not collect, receive, or store credit card details, billing addresses, or payment transaction tokens. All financial transactions are governed by [Apple's Media Services Terms and Conditions](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/).

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children of any age.

---

### 5. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the revised policy will be committed directly to this repository along with an updated effective date.
